[on arrival: The internet used to be people.] Some of you might remember what the internet used to be. It used to be people. You’d land on a forum where somebody had been to the place you were going, and they’d written it all down, badly, with typos, because they wanted you to have a good time. A blog by one person who’d fixed the exact thing that was broken on your car. You could feel a human on the other end. [click] Then, somewhere, it stopped. [click: the reviews] The bleach restaurant: four and a half stars, hundreds of them, all five words long, all on the same Tuesday. [click: the texts] The text message. Your parcel, your bank, your refund. [click: the article] The chicken nobody roasted. [click, click, click] Spam. Scams. Slop. Three words for one thing: the internet filled up with answers that no person stands behind.
